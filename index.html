Common questions

How do I add the keyboard?

Open Namefit and tap the keyboard button at the top of Home. It walks you through it: turn on the Namefit keyboard in Settings, then, if you like, allow Full Access. When you are typing anywhere, tap or hold the globe key to switch to Namefit.

What does Full Access do, and do I need it?

You do not need it to type. With Full Access on, the keyboard can read your pinned styles, your tape colour and whether you have Namefit Pro from the app. The keyboard has no network code and nothing you type leaves your device. To turn it on or off: Settings, Apps, Namefit, Keyboards, then Allow Full Access.

My styled name shows as boxes or plain letters somewhere

Some places accept only plain letters, and some phones cannot draw every styled letter. The checks under the tape tell you before you paste: Fits counts the line the way the place counts it, Shows says which phones can draw it, and Reads says how a screen reader will read it. Where a place takes plain letters only, copy the plain twin instead: hold Copy.

A place says my line is too long, but it looks short

Many styled letters are stored as two characters, and places count in different ways. Choose the place under the tape and the Fits check counts the way that place does. Where a place does not say how it counts, Namefit shows the range rather than a guess.

What does Namefit Pro add?

Every style, decoration and kaomoji, in the app and on the keyboard, every tape colour, and as many Kept items as you like. Without it you have Plain and a starter set of styles, the first few decorations and kaomoji of each kind, and every check.

I bought the full unlock years ago

It still unlocks everything, and Namefit Pro as well. Tap Restore in More, or on the Namefit Pro page, while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I move what I kept to a new device?

In Kept, tap Export and send the file to yourself. On the new device, open Kept and tap Import. Restoring your device from its backup brings Kept back too.
