What you pay for

Namefit styles and checks your text without a purchase: Plain and a starter set of styles, the first few decorations and kaomoji of each kind, every check, and the keyboard with its starter styles. Namefit Pro adds every style, decoration and kaomoji in the app and on the keyboard, every tape colour, and unlimited Kept items.

A subscription renews automatically until you cancel it, which you do in Settings on your device rather than in the app, at least a day before the current period ends. A one time purchase does not renew and there is nothing to cancel.

What Namefit is

It puts your text in styled Unicode letters, adds decorations and kaomoji, and checks the line against the place it is going: how that place counts it, which phones can draw it, and how a screen reader reads it. Each place's limit comes from that place's own published rules, with the date it was read.

What Namefit is not

It does not change the font of your phone or of another app; styled letters are characters, and the app or website you paste into decides whether to accept them. Places change their rules, and a place may refuse a line the app said would fit. The developer is not responsible for a line a place refuses, shortens or reports.
