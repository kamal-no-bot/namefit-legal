Privacy Policy

The short version: Namefit collects nothing. What you type and what you keep stay on your device.

What Namefit collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting service. There is no server belonging to this app, so there is nowhere for your information to go. Neither the app nor its keyboard sends anything you type or keep anywhere. The only connection the app makes is to the App Store, through Apple, to show prices, take a purchase and restore one.

The keyboard

The Namefit keyboard types without Full Access. It never stores, logs or sends what you type, and it has no network code. It does not read your clipboard.

Full Access is the name iOS gives a wider permission for keyboards. Namefit uses it for one thing only: so the keyboard can read, from the app on the same device, your pinned styles, your tape colour and whether you have Namefit Pro. If you leave it off, the keyboard still types its starter styles.

What stays on your device

  • The line you are styling, and the place you chose for it.
  • What you keep in Kept: lines, styles and kaomoji.
  • Lengths you add yourself, and the kaomoji you used recently.
  • Your settings, such as your tape colour and the styles pinned to the keyboard.
  • Whether you have Namefit Pro.

All of it is in the app's own storage on your device. There is no sync to any cloud service run by the developer. Like any app's data, it is part of your device's own backup if you have backups turned on. It leaves the device only if you export Kept yourself, as a file you choose where to send. The developer cannot see any of it.

Copying and reading aloud

Namefit puts a line on your clipboard only when you tap Copy. Reading a line aloud uses the voice built into iOS, on your device.

Shortcuts

The Style Text and Measure Text actions run on your device. The text you give them is not stored.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Namefit never sees your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have Namefit Pro, and that answer stays on your device.

Children

Namefit collects no data from anyone, of any age. There is no account to create, no profile, no messaging and no advertising.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear here and in the app's release notes. A version of Namefit that started sending what you type anywhere would be a different app from the one described here.
